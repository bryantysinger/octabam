Chooses what a muted or soloed-out audio track does. The default, OT, is stock's
behaviour.

## Access

PERSONALIZE > MUTE MODE, the second row, after PREVIEW WITHOUT FX. The
setting survives a power cycle.

## Modes

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| OT | stock: an instant cut after the FX |
| OTFX | the dry sound cuts, FX tails ring out, the sequencer keeps running |
| OTFX-T | as OTFX, new trigs suppressed |
| DT-T | the sounding note plays out on its amp envelope, new trigs suppressed |

## Notes

- A muted track that a COMPRESSOR uses as its KEY keeps feeding it, as on
  stock, including SIDECHAIN COMPRESSOR where the image has it.
