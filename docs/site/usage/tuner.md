A guitar-tuner readout of the current audio track: the note, the octave,
a ±50 cent needle and the frequency, taken from the track's post-FX,
pre-fader audio.

## Operation

Hold [UP] and press [TEMPO] to open the window. [TEMPO], [YES], [NO] or the
same chord closes it. [TEMPO] alone, [FUNC] + [TEMPO] and [UP] alone stay
as stock. Until the tuner has heard a steady note the window shows `--`.
